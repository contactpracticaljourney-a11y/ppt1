Why base Charges Different

Inductive Industrial Motors & Capacitor Bank Compensation

  1. KVAR like motors fans coils have high inductance high KVAR and LOW PF <=1 leads to more current requirement
  2. More inductive load in industry as compare to homes
  3. They have to Establishes Capacitor Bank to Bring Back the PF =1 else DHBVN charges Extra money
  4. Capacitor has PF >=1 due to Current lead and voltage lags and in inductor currentb lags behind and voltage leads
  5. KVA=V*I/1000
